# Torrance Yachts platform: client progress summary

**Date:** 15 July 2026

The project has moved from planning into a working, secured internal application. Four of twelve planned delivery phases have been completed and released.

## Completed

- A professional private web-application foundation with responsive desktop and mobile navigation.
- Individual staff login and protected internal pages.
- A structured yacht register containing operational details and yacht-specific notification information.
- A central contact and company directory for customers, brokers, captains, operations staff, suppliers, and other roles.
- The ability to assign reviewed captain, management, operations, crew, and notification responsibilities to each yacht.
- A structured manual enquiry pipeline linking customers, brokers, companies, yachts, dates, requirements, budgets, priorities, notes, and internal owners.
- Controlled enquiry status changes with mandatory reasons, permanent lifecycle history, workflow events, search, filtering, and live dashboard counts.
- Database-level access controls, validation, change attribution, audit history, automated tests, continuous integration, and maintained project documentation.

All development and demonstrations currently use synthetic information. No live client, broker, captain, contract, identity-document, banking, or confidential commercial data has been entered.

## Now in progress

Phase 5 adds editable yacht commercial profiles, seasonal rates, approved sales-material links, versioned response templates, and reviewable offers. Staff will be able to assemble an offer from current yacht data and enquiry requirements, review every term, and explicitly approve it before recording it as sent.

## Still to come

The later phases add yacht commercial profiles and offers, booking and availability controls, the full booking-task workflow, private document and payment handling, email ingestion, AI-assisted classification and drafting, notifications and calendar/Google integrations, followed by production security, backups, staff acceptance, training, and launch.

## Current status

The technical foundation and the first operational master-data modules are working and verified. The system is not yet approved for production or live data, and it does not yet process real enquiries, bookings, documents, emails, payments, or notifications. Development remains on the agreed dependency-safe path toward the complete enquiry-to-charter workflow.
