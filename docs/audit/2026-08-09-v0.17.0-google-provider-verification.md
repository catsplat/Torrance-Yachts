# v0.17.0 Google provider verification

Date: 9 August 2026

Scope: application-owned Google Calendar, Drive and Sheets delivery adapters,
shared staff OAuth selection and administrator resource selection.

## Acceptance evidence

- The approved staff Google connection was refreshed through normal Google
  consent with Calendar events and Drive file scopes added.
- The administrator selected that staff account for shared Google services.
- Calendar, Drive and Sheets provider operations remain individually disabled.
- The Drive folder and reporting spreadsheet pickers loaded authorised metadata;
  no destination was selected and no provider content was created or changed.
- Fake-provider tests cover Calendar update/create, private visibility, Sheet tab
  replacement, raw/formula-safe values, Drive replay detection and permission
  failure classification.
- PostgreSQL tests prove administrator-only account selection, required scopes,
  service-only credential decryption, immutable reporting identifiers and the
  content-only snapshot idempotency hash.

  reporting spreadsheet.

  operational records before any real charter data is mirrored.
