# Portfolio demonstration checklist

- Use the generated fictional administrator and rehearsal data only.
- Show the Inbox Review, Stale Enquiries, Booking Preparation and Overdue
  Payments views.
- Demonstrate access-pending isolation and the staff/administrator boundary.
- Walk one fictional email through AI review, enquiry, offer and booking.
- Show the 24-hour conflict, task dependencies and released-document boundary.
- Show invoice evidence, Yachtfolio evidence and post-charter follow-up.
- Reset the rehearsal and retain its redacted audit event.
- Capture no browser password manager, OAuth account picker, local token, private
  environment value or Supabase secret in screenshots.
- Crop screenshots to the application content and verify every visible person,
  company, yacht, contact, amount and document is fictional.
