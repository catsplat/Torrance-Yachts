# Torrance staff guide

Open the private application address, pass the company access screen, sign in and
complete the authenticator-code challenge. The **Work queue** is the normal daily
starting point.

- **Inbox review:** inspect categorisation, evidence, summary and proposed draft;
  correct or reject it before creating operational work.
- **Enquiries and offers:** maintain the customer request, yacht, dates and offer
  terms. Archive stale dead ends without deleting their history.
- **Booking preparation:** confirm a booking only after the signed agreement and
  extracted terms have been reviewed. The system blocks buffered date conflicts.
- **Tasks and documents:** follow dependencies 0.A through 7. Private files remain
  unavailable until validation, malware scan and release complete.
- **Payments and calendar:** record reviewed instalments and complete reminders;
  external calendars and Sheets are mirrors, not the source of truth.
- **Notifications:** configure personal routes in Settings. Telegram links return
  to an authenticated review page; consequential email becomes a Gmail draft.
- **AI settings:** choose the provider/model, test it, and select summary style.
  Provider output remains an editable review item.

Use **Settings > Tutorial** for the fictional end-to-end practice workflow. Contact
the administrator for access approval, MFA recovery, failed integrations or
quarantined documents.
